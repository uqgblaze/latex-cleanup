PowerPoint → Rise LaTeX Cleaner

Paste the LaTeX-like text copied from PowerPoint. The cleaner removes PowerPoint's extra escaping while preserving normal LaTeX commands such as \sum, \lambda, \cdot, and \frac.

Example

PowerPoint:
Z\_\{A\}\^\{\\ast\ \}\=\\sum\_\{i\=\1\}\^\{n\}\ \{\\lambda\ \}\_\{i\}\\cdot\ Z\_\{i\}

Result:
Z_{A}^{\ast} = \sum_{i = 1}^{n} {\lambda}_{i} \cdot Z_{i}
